\vspace{0.3cm}

%% ── Description of the work ──────────────────────────────
This reactive spatial sound installation represents an acoustically breathing gallery room -- or a fictional, abstract creature. In the illuminated centre of the space, a beam-forming array speaker builds a kind of column within which the intracorporeal sound of inhaled air is audible, while about a dozen loudspeaker chassis mounted on the walls project the sound of exhaled air, spreading through an imaginary larger building. The measured loudness of the gallery's inside and outside environment influences this acoustic creature and lets it react by temporarily falling quiet.

%% ── About the author ─────────────────────────────────────
\ruledtitle{About the Artist}

Robert Rehnig (born 1977) is a Leipzig-based sound artist and composer. He works in the fields of audio books, music for stage plays and sound installation art. His series ``Souvenirs entomologiques'' was awarded the German Audio Book Award. His works have been presented at (selection): Singuhr-H\"orgalerie Berlin, Festspielhaus Hellerau Dresden, Kunstverein Hamburg, ZixP-Festival Leipzig, Mid-Autumn Harvest Moon Festival Montr\'eal, Maison du G\'eant Salins-les-Bains, Patarei Tallinn, Festival Punto de Encuentro Valencia, sound:frame-Festival K\"unstlerhaus Vienna and Fr\"uhjahrstage f\"ur zeitgen\"ossische Musik Weimar. He teaches at SeaM (Studio for Electroacoustic Music and Sound Art, University of Music ``Franz Liszt'' Weimar) and at the electronic music studio of the University of Music and Theatre ``Felix Mendelssohn Bartholdy'' Leipzig. He is currently a PhD candidate at the Bauhaus-Universit\"at Weimar, researching the psychophysics and phenomenology of silence.
