# ITEM — Shuffled Time
### A seven-day performative week at RAA · Rīga · 5–11 July 2026

**Rīga, Latvia —** From **5 to 11 July 2026**, RAA opens its doors to *ITEM — Shuffled Time*, a seven-day performative week that hands the space to a different artist every single day. It is a format still rarely seen in Rīga — and, in this durational, round-the-clock shape, an unusual proposition anywhere: seven international artists, seven consecutive 24-hour windows, one continuous meditation on time, space, and presence.

Each day, one artist or group receives a full **24-hour window** — from 9:00 in the morning to 9:00 the next day — to **I**nvoke, **T**emper, **E**late, **M**int the space. What happens inside that window is entirely up to them. Some works run for a few concentrated hours; others stretch toward an open end. The public is invited to arrive at any time, stay as long as they like, and leave whenever they choose. On each day there is also one **scheduled group visit**, a moment when everyone gathers together.

The title says it plainly: time here is *shuffled*. Across the week the acts reorder chronology, expand and contract duration, and treat the same 138 m² room as a shared instrument. The programme moves across durational performance, body art, experimental poetry, aromagraphy (scent as artistic language), and interactive installation — each performance standing on its own while forming part of a larger, week-long argument about how we experience time.

*ITEM — Shuffled Time* is hosted and organized by **RAA**, an independent cultural space in Rīga and part of the Veldze creative quarter (Radošā Rūpnīca Veldze). It brings together artists from **Latvia, the Netherlands, and Croatia**.

### The acts in brief

- **Words to Change the World** — Ilze Aulmane (LV). An invitation to question, challenge, and reflect: do we have the power to change the world, and are there words capable of doing so? A space built from breath, sound, letters, syllables, unfamiliar words, and performative objects. *Group visit 19:30.* → [raa.space/item/words-to-change-the-world](https://raa.space/item/words-to-change-the-world)

- **Now** — Maruta Gredzena (LV). An interactive performance in which visitors choose and wear light, paper-based wearable sculptures, becoming both observers and artwork. Can honesty exist without language? *Group visit 19:00.* → [raa.space/item/now](https://raa.space/item/now)

- **N.3** — Ernests Ā (LV). A silent, durational action with a rope and a column, repeated until exhaustion — a playful, unyielding exploration of freedom and constraint. *Group visit 19:00.* → [raa.space/item/N_3](https://raa.space/item/N_3)

- **Gluttony** — Kristiāna Šuksta (LV) & Sandris Muriņš (LV). An experimental *aromagraphy* performance pairing twelve words with twelve scents, continuously reshuffling their order to explore desire, memory, and the sensory dimensions of consumption. *Group visit 20:00.* → [raa.space/item/gluttony](https://raa.space/item/gluttony)

- **Presence in Absence Piece Three** — Lisette Ros (NL). Part of an ongoing research series on the absent body: the artist manipulates her own presence and consciousness in real time, before the audience. *Group visit 18:00.* → [raa.space/item/presenceinabsence_piecethree](https://raa.space/item/presenceinabsence_piecethree)

- **Time Scores** — Vanessa Brzić (HR). Inspired by Fluxus event scores, the audience draws and performs short instructions that gradually transform the room — treating the space as a metaphor for social and historical time. *Group visit 19:00.* → [raa.space/item/timescores](https://raa.space/item/timescores)

- **Laiks telpā** — Ilze Mazpane (LV) & Jana Zariņa (LV). A durational performance that turns the existing RAA space and its objects into a musical, physical, and spatial experience of subjective time — how it expands, contracts, rushes, comes to a standstill, and disappears. *Group visit 18:00.* → [raa.space/item/laiks_telpa](https://raa.space/item/laiks_telpa)

## About RAA

RAA is an independent cultural space in Rīga hosting regular theatrical and contemporary performances, exhibitions, and workshops. Located at Matīsa iela 8 within the Veldze creative quarter, its main hall spans 138 m² with ten large windows onto the street. At RAA you don't have to be loud to be heard. The name comes from *Reductio ad Absurdum* — an argument that establishes a claim by showing that its contrary leads to absurdity.
